Project of the Day: Integrating Randomness!

For this project, I created Had to come up with a way to integrate Randomness in some way into the game, all the while being unable to look anything up, use any generative AI, or help from peers. It was a pure test of skill.

So what I decided to do was make a drawing challenge! Starting the game you only got 2 minutes to look at the generated prompt, choose your color, pen size, pen opacity, and get to drawing! The list of Nouns, Verbs, and Adjectives goes on for quite a while, so there's lots of replayability.
